“Writing gives my imagination a place to exist.”

I start with a thought.

Then a question.

Then someone appears — a character who did not exist a moment ago.

A WORLD FOLLOWS.

Writing lets simple ideas become places, people, memories, and impossible things.

Sometimes music becomes the doorway. Sometimes an image. Sometimes just a sentence that refuses to leave.

The page is where I let them stay.
